# 🌑 Shadowbound: Dark Relics

![Minecraft 1.20.1](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.20.1-brightgreen)
![Loader](https://img.shields.io/badge/Modloader-Fabric-blue)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-purple)

A Dark Fantasy weapons, cursed armors, and occult relics mod for **Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric)**.

---

## 🗡️ Features & What's Included

### 1. Dark Fantasy Weapons
* **Bloodthorn Greatsword:** 
  * *Ability (Lifeleech):* Restores the wielder's health on every strike.
  * *Debuff (Hemorrhage):* Inflicts decaying Wither and Slowness on victims.
* **Harvester of the Hollow (Soul Scythe):**
  * *Ability (Soul Surge):* Reaps enemy spirits, granting a surge of Speed II and a golden Absorption shield.
  * *Debuff (Shroud):* Blinds and withers the target in occult smoke.
* **Shadowfang Dagger:**
  * *Ability (Shadow Ambush):* Striking from stealth or dark areas (light < 7) inflicts Blindness and lingering Wither.
* **Grimsteel Claymore:**
  * Heavy two-handed dark iron greatsword with sweeping strikes and high base durability.

### 2. Cursed Armor Sets
* **Grimsteel Armor Set (Visor, Cuirass, Greaves, Sabatons):**
  * Forged from dark obsidian steel with glowing cyan runes.
  * *Set Bonus (Obsidian Bastion):* Full set grants continuous **Night Vision** and **Resistance I**.
* **Bloodforged Armor Set (Crown, Hauberk, Chausses, Striders):**
  * Gothic crimson plates with gilded trim and a glowing chest gem.
  * *Set Bonus (Blood Frenzy):* Complete immunity to the **Wither** effect. When your health drops below 50%, activates **Blood Frenzy** (Strength II + Speed I).

### 3. Occult Crafting Materials
* **Shadow Remnant:** Condensed dark essence harvested from Crying Obsidian, Nether Wart, and Coal.
* **Grimsteel Ingot:** Heavy dark alloy forged by infusing Netherite scrap with Shadow Remnants.
* **Blood Gem:** A pulsating crimson ruby crystallized with redstone power.
* **Void Essence:** A rare cosmic drop of pure darkness.

### 4. Quality of Life & In-Game Features
* **Hold [Shift] for Lore & Abilities:** Hovering normally shows clean, compact stats. Holding **Shift** unfolds the full dark fantasy lore and ability breakdown.
* **Recipe Book Integration (📗):** All recipes automatically unlock in your Crafting Table's green Recipe Book on world join, complete with page navigation under the Equipment tab.
* **Custom Creative Tab:** A dedicated inventory tab titled `Shadowbound: Dark Relics` with all items and gear.

## 📥 How to Download & Install

### Requirements
* **Minecraft Version:** `1.20.1`
* **Mod Loader:** [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) (v0.15.0 or newer)
* **Required Dependency:** [Fabric API 1.20.1](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)

### Installation Steps (TLauncher / Prism / CurseForge / Vanilla)
1. Download the latest **`shadowbound-1.0.0.jar`** from the [Releases](https://github.com/hananOanunu/shadowbound/releases) tab.
2. Download **`Fabric API`** for Minecraft 1.20.1.
3. Open your Minecraft or TLauncher folder:
   * **In TLauncher:** Click the little yellow folder icon 📁 at the bottom right.
   * **On Windows:** Press `Win + R`, type `%appdata%\.minecraft\mods`, and press Enter.
   * **On Mac:** Press `Cmd + Shift + G` in Finder and enter `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/mods`.
4. Put both `.jar` files into the **`mods`** folder.
5. In your launcher, select version **Fabric 1.20.1** (or your Fabric profile) and click **Play / Enter the game**!

## 🔨 Crafting Guide

All recipes are crafted on a standard **Crafting Table**:

### Materials
* **Shadow Remnant (x4):** `[Crying Obsidian] + [Nether Wart] + [Coal]` (Shapeless)
* **Grimsteel Ingot:** 1 Netherite Scrap in the center surrounded by 4 Shadow Remnants (Plus shape).
* **Blood Gem:** 1 Diamond in the center surrounded by 4 Redstone Blocks (Plus shape).

### Weapons
* **Bloodthorn Greatsword:** 2 Blood Gems stacked vertically over 1 Grimsteel Ingot.
* **Soul Scythe:** 2 Grimsteel Ingots across the top row, with 2 Sticks below.
* **Grimsteel Claymore:** 2 Grimsteel Ingots stacked vertically over 1 Stick.
* **Shadowfang Dagger:** 1 Shadow Remnant over 1 Grimsteel Ingot over 1 Stick.

### Armor
* **Grimsteel Armor:** 5/8/7/4 Grimsteel Ingots crafted in standard Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots shapes.
* **Bloodforged Armor:** 5/8/7/4 Blood Gems crafted in standard Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots shapes.
